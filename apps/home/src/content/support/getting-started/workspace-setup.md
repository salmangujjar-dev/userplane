A workspace is where your team manages recording links, reviews recordings, and configures settings. Every Userplane account can create up to 3 workspaces.

## Create a workspace

1. After signing in, click **Create Workspace**.
2. You'll be prompted to enter your **Company URL**.
3. Once created, you can invite teammates and connect integrations like Intercom.

Post creation, you become the **owner** of the workspace and can manage all settings.

## Invite teammates

1. Go to **Workspace Settings > Team**.
2. Click **Invite Member**.
3. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite.
4. If you've connected Intercom, you'll see recommendations from your Intercom workspace.
5. Click **Send Invite**.

Invited users will receive an email with a link to join. See [Team Management](/support/workspace/team-management) for more on managing members.

## Connect Intercom

With Userplane's Intercom integration, your support agents can create recording links and view recordings directly from conversations.

1. Go to **Workspace Settings > Integrations**.
2. Click **Connect Intercom**.
3. You'll be redirected to Intercom to authorize the connection.
4. Userplane requests permissions to read conversations and manage the inbox sidebar app.
5. After authorizing, you'll be redirected back to Userplane and the connection is active.

See [Intercom Setup](/support/getting-started/intercom-setup) for more details on what happens when you connect and how to invite Intercom admins.

## Configure your invite policy

Your workspace invite policy controls how new people can join:

- **Private** — only explicit invitations work. Your workspace isn't discoverable.
- **Whitelisted** — people with matching email domains can join directly.
- **Protected** — people with matching email domains can request access, which you'll need to approve.

You can whitelist up to **5 email domains**. See [Invite Policies](/support/workspace/invite-policies) for more details.
