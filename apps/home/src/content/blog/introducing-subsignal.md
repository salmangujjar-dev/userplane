# Introducing Subsignal

In the fast-paced world of venture capital and private equity, timing is everything. Missing a crucial market move or a competitor's pivot can mean the difference between a successful investment and a missed opportunity.

## The Challenge

Investors today face an overwhelming amount of information. Tracking companies you've passed on, monitoring your sector thesis, and staying current with competitive intelligence requires constant vigilance and significant resources.

## Our Solution

Subsignal automates competitive intelligence and market monitoring, helping you:

- **Never miss a market move**: Automated monitoring of companies and competitors
- **Time your bets better**: Track companies you passed on and re-engage at the right moment
- **Maintain your sector thesis**: Stay current with sector trends and movements
- **Be the investor founders turn to**: Position yourself as the most informed investor in your space

## Key Features

### Market Intelligence

Get a leg up with the sectors you're most interested in. Every change, no matter how small, gets flagged.

### Deal Intelligence

Time your bets better. Track companies you passed on and re-engage when they hit key milestones.

### Relationship Intelligence

Stay connected through pivots and false starts. Monitor passed opportunities for breakthrough moments.

### Competitive Intelligence

Track competitive threats to your portfolio companies. Whether it's roadmap updates, pricing shifts, or positioning plays, be the first to know.
