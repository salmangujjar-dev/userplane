# Best Practices for Competitive Intelligence

Competitive intelligence is essential for staying ahead in today's market. Here's how to do it effectively.

## 1. Define Your Monitoring Scope

Start by identifying:

- Key competitors in your sector
- Portfolio companies' competitive landscape
- Adjacent markets that could disrupt your thesis
- Emerging players to watch

## 2. Establish Monitoring Cadence

Different aspects require different monitoring frequencies:

- **Daily**: Major announcements, funding rounds, leadership changes
- **Weekly**: Product updates, blog posts, marketing campaigns
- **Monthly**: Strategic shifts, market positioning, hiring trends
- **Quarterly**: Financial performance, market share changes

## 3. Automate Where Possible

Manual monitoring doesn't scale. Automate:

- Website change detection
- Social media monitoring
- News and press release tracking
- Job posting analysis
- Pricing and product page monitoring

## 4. Focus on Signals, Not Noise

Not all changes are meaningful. Focus on:

- Strategic pivots
- Product launches
- Major partnerships
- Leadership changes
- Funding events
- Market expansions

## 5. Maintain Context

Competitive intelligence is only valuable with context:

- Track changes over time
- Maintain historical records
- Note market conditions
- Document your analysis
- Share insights with your team

## 6. Act on Intelligence

Intelligence without action is wasted effort:

- Set up alert thresholds
- Define action triggers
- Assign ownership for follow-ups
- Schedule regular reviews
- Measure impact of insights

## 7. Stay Compliant

Always ensure your competitive intelligence gathering:

- Uses only public information
- Respects privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA)
- Follows ethical guidelines
- Documents sources
- Maintains transparency

## Conclusion

Effective competitive intelligence requires the right balance of automation, human insight, and systematic processes. With proper implementation, it becomes a strategic advantage that compounds over time.

Start with one or two key areas, prove the value, then expand your monitoring scope as you build confidence and capabilities.
