The usage dashboard in Workspace Settings shows you how much of each resource your workspace has consumed in the current billing period.

## Where to find usage

Go to [Workspace Settings > Billing](https://dash.userplane.io/_/settings?tab=plan-usage) to see the usage section. Each resource shows:

- A **progress bar** indicating how much has been used.
- A **percentage** of the limit consumed.
- The **current count** vs the plan limit.

## Usage warning levels

As your usage increases, Userplane shows different warning levels:

| Level             | Meaning                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Safe**          | Usage is well within your plan limits. No action needed.                                                     |
| **Soft warning**  | You are approaching your soft limit. Consider monitoring usage or planning an upgrade.                       |
| **Soft exceeded** | You have passed the soft limit. You can still use the resource but are approaching the hard cap.             |
| **Hard warning**  | You are close to the hard limit. Upgrade soon to avoid interruption.                                         |
| **Hard exceeded** | The hard limit has been reached. The resource is blocked until the next billing period or until you upgrade. |

## Notifications

If [usage notifications](/help/account/notification-preferences) are enabled, you receive email alerts when your workspace enters the warning or exceeded states.

## What to do when limits are reached

1. **Upgrade your plan** — move to a higher tier with more resources. See [Upgrading Your Plan](/help/billing/upgrading-your-plan).
2. **Wait for the next billing period** — period-based resources (recordings, links, screenshots, files) reset at the start of each cycle.
3. **Remove unused members** — if you are at the seat limit, removing inactive members frees up seats.
