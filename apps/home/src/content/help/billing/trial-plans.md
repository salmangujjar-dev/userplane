Every new Userplane workspace starts with a 14-day trial. During the trial you get full access to all features, so you can evaluate the product before choosing a plan.

## What is included

The trial gives you the same experience as a paid plan. All features are available, including recordings, recording links, console and network capture, custom metadata, and branding.

You cannot upgrade, downgrade, or purchase add-ons during the trial.

## Trial duration

The trial lasts **14 days** from the date your workspace is created. A countdown is displayed in Workspace Settings so you always know how much time remains.

## Requesting an extension

Need more time to evaluate Userplane? Open a [support ticket](/help/support/contact-us) and let us know. We are happy to extend your trial on a case-by-case basis.

## What happens when the trial ends

When the 14-day trial expires, your workspace reverts to the **Free** plan limits. All your recordings, links, and data are preserved — nothing is deleted. Choose a paid plan to restore full access.

## Choosing a plan after trial

Once the trial ends (or whenever you are ready), pick a plan from [Workspace Settings > Billing](https://dash.userplane.io/_/settings?tab=plan-usage&plan-modal=detailed). Compare plans on the [Subscription Overview](/help/billing/subscription-overview) page, then follow the steps in [Upgrading Your Plan](/help/billing/upgrading-your-plan) to complete checkout.
