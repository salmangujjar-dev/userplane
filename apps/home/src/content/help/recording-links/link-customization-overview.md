Userplane gives you two ways to customize the recording experience for your customers:

1. **Global branding** — set your company logo, name, colors, and text that appear on all recording pages.
2. **Per-domain recording preferences** — control what data is captured (console logs, network activity, client actions) for each verified domain.

## Global branding

Branding settings apply to all recording links in your workspace. You can configure:

- Company logo and name
- Recording page header and footnote text
- Primary color
- Enable or disable branding entirely

See [Branding Your Links](/help/recording-links/branding-your-links) for a full walkthrough.

## Per-domain recording preferences

Each verified domain can have its own settings for what data is captured alongside the video:

- Hide sensitive fields
- Capture console logs
- Capture client actions
- Capture network logs
- Capture backend logs
- Capture voice and device audio

These preferences only take effect after the domain is [verified](/help/workspace/domain-verification). See [Domain Recording Preferences](/help/recording-links/domain-recording-preferences) for details.

## How they work together

Branding controls the **look and feel** of the recording page. Domain preferences control **what data is collected**. Both are configured in Workspace Settings and apply automatically to recording links using that domain.
