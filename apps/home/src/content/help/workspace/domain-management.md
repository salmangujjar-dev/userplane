The Domains section in Workspace Settings lets you manage all domains in your workspace.

## Viewing domains

Go to **Workspace Settings > Domains** to see all domains. Each domain shows its name, status (pending, verified, or revoked), and creation date.

## Sorting

Sort your domain list by name, status, or creation date to find what you need.

## Editing a domain

Select a domain to view its details. From here you can:

- View the current verification status.
- Trigger re-verification if the domain status is pending or revoked.
- Access [domain recording preferences](/help/recording-links/domain-recording-preferences) for verified domains.

## Deleting a domain

1. Select the domain you want to delete.
2. Click **Delete**.

Deleting a domain removes it from your workspace. Existing recording links that use this domain will no longer be branded, but the links and recordings themselves are preserved.
