The Intercom integration lets your support team request and review screen recordings directly from Intercom conversations. Agents can create recording links, view recordings, and link them to conversations without switching tools.

## What the integration does

- Installs a **sidebar app** in the Intercom inbox for creating links and viewing recordings.
- Associates recordings with Intercom conversations automatically.
- Supports using [reusable recording links](/help/recording-links/reusable-recording-links) in [Intercom macros](/help/integrations/intercom-macros).

## Connecting Intercom

1. Go to **Workspace Settings > Integrations**.
2. Click **Connect Intercom**.
3. Authorize the connection in Intercom. Userplane requests permissions to read conversations and manage the inbox sidebar app.
4. After authorization, the sidebar app is installed in your Intercom inbox.

Only workspace **owners** and **admins** can connect the integration.

## Permissions granted

When you authorize the Intercom connection, Userplane receives the following permissions:

- Read conversation data to associate recordings with conversations.
- Install and manage the inbox sidebar app.

## Disconnecting Intercom

1. Go to **Workspace Settings > Integrations**.
2. Click **Disconnect** next to Intercom.

Disconnecting removes the sidebar app from Intercom. Existing recordings and links are preserved.
