After [connecting Intercom](/help/integrations/intercom-integration), a Userplane sidebar app appears in your Intercom inbox. This app lets agents create recording links, view recordings, and associate them with conversations — all without leaving Intercom.

## Creating a recording link

1. Open a conversation in the Intercom inbox.
2. Click the **Userplane** sidebar app.
3. Click **Create Link**.
4. Select a **project** from the dropdown.
5. Select a **domain** from the dropdown.
6. Click **Create** and send the link to the customer in the conversation.

## Viewing recordings

When a customer completes a recording from a link created in an Intercom conversation:

1. Open the conversation in the Intercom inbox.
2. Click the **Userplane** sidebar app.
3. Recordings associated with the conversation appear in the sidebar.
4. Click a recording to view details or open it in Userplane for full playback.

## Conversation linking

Recordings created through the Intercom sidebar app are automatically linked to the Intercom conversation. This makes it easy to find the recording later from either Userplane or Intercom.

## Using Cmd+K instead

If you prefer keyboard shortcuts, you can also create recording links using the Intercom Cmd+K action menu. Press **Cmd+K** (or **Ctrl+K** on Windows), type **Userplane**, and follow the prompts. See [Intercom Shortcuts](/help/integrations/intercom-shortcuts) for details.

## Requirements

- The Intercom integration must be [connected](/help/integrations/intercom-integration).
- The agent must be a member of the Userplane workspace.
- Domains must be [verified](/help/workspace/domain-verification) to appear in the domain dropdown.
