---
title: Updating Your Profile
description: Change your display name and other profile settings
---

Your profile settings control how you appear to other workspace members and to customers who receive recording links from you.

## Changing your display name

Your display name is shown to:

- Other workspace members in the team list and activity feeds.
- Customers in recording links (the "requested by" field).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Go to [Account Settings > Profile](https://dash.userplane.io/_/account).

    <Frame caption="Account settings">
      <img src="/media/account/account-settings.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your name">
    Edit the **Display name** field and save your changes.

    <Frame caption="Account profile settings">
      <img src="/media/account/account-profile.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Profile settings

From Account Settings you can also manage:

- **Email address** — the email associated with your Userplane account.
  <Frame caption="Account email address">
    <img src="/media/account/account-profile.png" />
  </Frame>
- **Workspace memberships** — view and manage the workspaces you belong to. See [Leaving a Workspace](/account/leaving-workspace).

<Frame caption="Workspace memberships">
  <img src="/media/account/account-workspaces.png" />
</Frame>

- **Notification preferences** — control which notifications you receive. See [Notification Preferences](/account/notifications).

<Frame caption="Notification preferences">
  <img src="/media/account/workspaces-notification-preference.png" />
</Frame>
