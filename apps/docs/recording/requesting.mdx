---
title: Requesting a Recording
description: Create a recording link and share it with your customer to capture a screen recording
---

A recording starts with a link. You create a recording link in Userplane, share it with your customer, and they record their screen directly in the browser — no installs required.

## Create a recording link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Create Link dialog">
    Click the [Create Link](https://dash.userplane.io/_/recordings?create_link=true) button in the
    top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the link details">
    - **Project** — choose which project this recording belongs to. This helps you organize and
    filter recordings later. - **Domain** — select a verified domain to brand the recording link
    with your site's identity. Optional but recommended. - **Reference** — add an external reference
    like a ticket number or URL (e.g. `JIRA-456`, a GitHub issue link). This makes it easy to find
    the recording later. - **Reusable** — toggle whether the link can be used once or multiple
    times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy and share">Click **Create** and copy the link.</Step>
</Steps>

## Share the link

Send the recording link to your customer through any channel — email, live chat, Intercom, or your help desk. When the customer opens the link, they see a branded recording page and can start recording immediately.

<Tip>
  If you use Intercom or Zendesk, you can create and send recording links directly from the
  [Intercom inbox app](/integrations/intercom-inbox) or the [Zendesk ticket
  sidebar](/integrations/zendesk-ticket-sidebar).
</Tip>

## What happens next

After the customer finishes recording, the recording appears in your [Recordings](https://dash.userplane.io/_/recordings) page. Incoming recording notifications are opt-in. To receive email alerts when recordings arrive, enable "Incoming recordings" in your [notification preferences](/account/notifications).

## Sharing with non-members

You can send a recording link to anyone, including people outside your workspace. However, they need to join the workspace to view the recording. The recording link itself is open to any recipient for recording purposes.
