---
title: Requesting a Recording
description: Create a recording link and share it with your customer to capture a screen recording
---

A recording starts with a link. You create a recording link in Userplane, share it with your customer, and they record their screen directly in the browser — no installs required.

## Create a recording link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Create Link dialog">
    Click the [Create Link](https://dash.userplane.io/_/recordings?create_link=true) button in the
    top right corner.

    <Frame caption="Create Link button">
      <img src="/media/links/create-link/create-link.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the link details">
    - **Project** — choose which project this recording belongs to. This helps you organize and
    filter recordings later.
    - **Domain** — select a verified domain to brand the recording link
    with your site's identity. Optional but recommended.
    - **Reference** — add an external reference
    like a ticket number or URL (e.g. `JIRA-456`, a GitHub issue link). This makes it easy to find
    the recording later.
    - **Reusable** — toggle whether the link can be used once or multiple
    times.

    <Frame caption="Create Link form">
      <img src="/media/links/create-link/create-link-unfilled.png" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Project selection">
      <img src="/media/links/create-link/create-link-project-selection.png" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Adding a reference">
      <img src="/media/links/create-link/create-link-link-reference.png" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Reusable link toggle">
      <img src="/media/links/create-link/create-link-reusable.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy and share">Click **Create** and copy the link.</Step>
</Steps>

## Share the link

Send the recording link to your customer through any channel — email, live chat, Intercom, or your help desk. When the customer opens the link, they see a branded recording page and can start recording immediately.

<Frame caption="Share link modal">
  <img src="/media/links/share-link/share-link-modal.png" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  You can also create recording links from the [Chrome
  extension](/integrations/chrome-extension-create-links) side panel, or directly inside your help
  desk using the [Intercom inbox app](/integrations/intercom-inbox) or the [Zendesk ticket
  sidebar](/integrations/zendesk-ticket-sidebar).
</Tip>

## What happens next

After the customer opens the link, they go through the following flow. The finished recording appears in your [Recordings](https://dash.userplane.io/_/recordings) page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Customer opens the link">
    The customer sees a branded recording page and can start recording immediately — no installs required.

    <Frame caption="Customer recording page">
      <img src="/media/recorder/recorder-get-started.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Recording in progress">
    The customer's screen is captured in real time along with console logs, network activity, and user actions.

    <Frame caption="Recording in progress">
      <img src="/media/recorder/recording-in-progress.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before uploading">
    After stopping the recording, the customer can review the capture and add notes to describe the issue.

    <Frame caption="Review recording before upload">
      <img src="/media/recorder/review-recording-prior-upload.png" />
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Adding notes before upload">
      <img src="/media/recorder/add-notes-prior-upload.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload completes">
    The recording is uploaded and immediately available in your workspace.

    <Frame caption="Upload in progress">
      <img src="/media/recorder/recording-upload-inprogress.png" />

<Tip>
  Incoming recording notifications are opt-in. To receive email alerts when recordings arrive,
  enable "Incoming recordings" in your [notification preferences](/account/notifications).
</Tip>

## Creating links from the Chrome extension

If you prefer to stay in the browser rather than switching to the dashboard, the [Chrome extension](/integrations/chrome-extension) lets you create recording links from a side panel alongside any tab. Press `Cmd+Shift+Y` (Mac) / `Ctrl+Shift+Y` (Windows/Linux) to open the Create Link form — the reference field auto-populates with the current page URL.

This is especially useful when you're already looking at a help desk ticket or customer page. See [Chrome Extension Link Creation](/integrations/chrome-extension-create-links) for the full walkthrough.
