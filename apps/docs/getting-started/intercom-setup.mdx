---
title: Intercom Setup
description: Connect Userplane to Intercom so your team can request recordings from inside conversations
---

Userplane integrates with Intercom through OAuth, so your support agents can create recording links and view recordings without ever leaving the Intercom inbox.

## Connect Intercom

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations settings">
    Go to [Workspace Settings >
    Integrations](https://dash.userplane.io/_/settings?tab=integrations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Intercom">
    Click **Connect Intercom**. You'll be redirected to Intercom to authorize the connection.

    <Frame caption="Connect Intercom">
      <img src="/media/intercom/workspace-settings/pre-connection-modal.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Userplane requests permissions to read conversations and manage the inbox sidebar app.

    <Frame caption="Authorize Intercom">
      <img src="/media/intercom/intercom-oauth/intercom-oauth-consent.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    After authorizing, you'll be redirected back to Userplane and the connection is active.

    <Frame caption="Connection successful">
      <img src="/media/intercom/intercom-oauth/intercom-oauth-successful.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Only workspace **owners** and **admins** can manage the Intercom integration.</Note>

## What happens when you connect

- Userplane is installed in your **Intercom inbox**. Your agents can create recording links and view recordings directly from conversations.
- **Intercom shortcuts** are added to your workspace, so agents can request screen recordings from customers in just a couple of clicks directly inside Intercom.
- If Userplane isn't pinned to your sidebar, be sure to add it so it's easy to access. To do so, go to your Intercom inbox, select **Edit app**, and pin Userplane.

## Invite Intercom admins

Once connected, you'll be prompted to invite your Intercom admins as workspace members so they can use Userplane inside Intercom:

<Frame caption="Invite teammates">
  <img src="/media/intercom/intercom-oauth/post-onboarding-invite-teammates.png" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select members to invite">
    Select which Intercom members you want to invite and click [Invite
    Member](https://dash.userplane.io/_/recordings?invite=true).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter email addresses">
    You can also enter the email addresses of your workspace members directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invitations">
    Invited users will receive an email invitation to join your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally whitelist domains">
    Whitelist specific email domains to enable org-wide access. Approved users can request and
    review customer recordings without you having to invite each person individually.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disconnect Intercom

1. Go to [Workspace Settings > Integrations](https://dash.userplane.io/_/settings?tab=integrations).
2. Click **Disconnect** next to the Intercom integration.

<Note>
  Disconnecting will make Userplane inoperable within Intercom. Your existing recordings and links
  won't be affected.
</Note>
