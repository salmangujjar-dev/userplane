When you [connect Slack](/integrations/slack), a **Userplane** tab appears in the Apps section of your Slack sidebar. The App Home gives you a personal dashboard — recent recordings, recent links, and an inline form for creating new ones — without leaving Slack.

## Dashboard overview

Open the Userplane app in Slack and click the **Home** tab. You'll see:

- **Recent Recordings** — the three most recent recordings in your workspace, with thumbnails and links to open them in Userplane.
- **Recent Links** — the three most recent recording links you've created.
- **Create Link** — a button to open the inline link creation form.

  <img src="/media/slack/home/home-connected.png" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  The App Home shows data specific to your account. If your email isn't matched to a workspace
  member, you'll see an access prompt instead.
</Note>

## Creating a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create Link">
    In the App Home, click **Create Link**.

    <Frame caption="Create Link button in App Home">
      <img src="/media/slack/home/home-create-link-button.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a project and domain">
    Choose the **Project** and **Domain** from the dropdowns. Your last selection is remembered for the next time.

    <Frame caption="Inline create link form">
      <img src="/media/slack/home/home-create-link-form.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    Click **Create**. The link is generated and the App Home updates to show a success view with the new link URL.

    <Frame caption="Link created — success view">
      <img src="/media/slack/home/home-link-created-success.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Browsing recordings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click View All in the Recordings section">
    From the App Home, click **View All** under Recent Recordings to open the full browse view.

    <Frame caption="Browse recordings view">
      <img src="/media/slack/home/home-browse-recordings.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Use the filter dropdowns to narrow by **Project**, **Domain**, or **Date Range** (last 7 days, last 30 days, or all time).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Go">
    Click **Go** to apply your filters. Results update in the same view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate pages">
    Use the **Next** and **Previous** buttons to move through paginated results (10 recordings per page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a recording">
    Click any recording to open it in Userplane for full playback and inspection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Browsing links

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click View All in the Links section">
    From the App Home, click **View All** under Recent Links.

    <Frame caption="Browse links view">
      <img src="/media/slack/home/home-browse-links.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Filter by **Project**, **Domain**, or **Status** (active, expired, or all).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Go">
    Click **Go** to apply your filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate pages">
    Use **Next** and **Previous** to page through results.
  </Step>
</Steps>
