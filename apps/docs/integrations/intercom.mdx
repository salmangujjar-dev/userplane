title: Intercom Integration
description: Connect Userplane to Intercom for in-conversation screen recording requests

The Intercom integration lets your support team request and review screen recordings directly from Intercom conversations. Agents can create recording links, view recordings, and link them to conversations without switching tools.

## What the integration does

- Installs a **sidebar app** in the Intercom inbox for creating links and viewing recordings.
- Adds a **Cmd+K shortcut** so agents can type "Userplane" in the action menu to create recording links without navigating away from a conversation.
- Associates recordings with Intercom conversations automatically.
- Posts an **admin note** to the conversation when a customer completes a recording.
- Supports using [reusable recording links](/recording-links/reusable) in [Intercom macros](/integrations/intercom-macros).

## Connecting Intercom

There are two ways to connect Userplane to Intercom:

<Note>Only workspace **owners** and **admins** can connect the integration.</Note>

      </Step>
      <Step title="Create a workspace">

        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authorization complete">

## Permissions granted

When you authorize the Intercom connection, Userplane receives the following permissions:

- Read conversation data to associate recordings with conversations.
- Install and manage the inbox sidebar app.

## Disconnecting Intercom

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations settings">
    Go to [Workspace Settings > Integrations](https://dash.userplane.io/_/settings?tab=integrations).

    <Frame caption="Integrations — connected">
      <img src="/media/intercom/workspace-settings/post-connection-modal.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Disconnect">
    Click **Disconnect** next to Intercom and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Disconnecting removes the sidebar app from Intercom. Existing recordings and links are preserved.
</Note>
