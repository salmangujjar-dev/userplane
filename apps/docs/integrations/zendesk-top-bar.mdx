---
title: Zendesk Top Bar
description: Create recording links from anywhere in Zendesk using the top bar app
---

The top bar app gives you quick access to Userplane from any page in Zendesk — not just tickets. Use it to create recording links before navigating to a specific ticket, or to generate links you want to share outside of Zendesk.

## Creating a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the top bar app">Click the **Userplane** icon in the Zendesk top bar.</Step>
  <Step title="Request a recording">
    Click **Request Recording**, then select a **project** and **domain** from the dropdowns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and copy">
    Click **Create Link**. Copy the link URL to share it via email, Slack, or any other channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Since the top bar is not associated with a specific ticket, no ticket context (ID, subject,
  requester) is captured. For ticket-specific links, use the [ticket
  sidebar](/integrations/zendesk-ticket-sidebar) or [ticket
  editor](/integrations/zendesk-ticket-editor) instead.
</Note>

## When to use the top bar

- **Pre-creating links** — generate a recording link before starting a conversation.
- **Sharing outside Zendesk** — copy a link to send via email, Slack, or any channel outside of Zendesk.
- **Quick access** — create a link without navigating to a specific ticket.

## Requirements

- The Zendesk integration must be [connected](/integrations/zendesk).
- The agent must be a member of the Userplane workspace.
- Domains must be [verified](/workspace/domain-verification) to appear in the domain dropdown.
