---
title: Using Userplane in Intercom
description: Create recording links and view recordings from the Intercom inbox sidebar
---

After [connecting Intercom](/integrations/intercom), a Userplane sidebar app appears in your Intercom inbox. This app lets agents create recording links, view recordings, and associate them with conversations — all without leaving Intercom.

## Creating a recording link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a conversation">Open a conversation in the Intercom inbox.</Step>
  <Step title="Open the sidebar app">Click the **Userplane** sidebar app.</Step>
  <Step title="Create the link">
    Click **Create Link**, select a **project** and **domain** from the dropdowns, then click
    **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to customer">Send the link to the customer in the conversation.</Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing recordings

When a customer completes a recording from a link created in an Intercom conversation:

1. Open the conversation in the Intercom inbox.
2. Click the **Userplane** sidebar app.
3. Recordings associated with the conversation appear in the sidebar.
4. Click a recording to view details or open it in Userplane for full playback.

## Conversation linking

Recordings created through the Intercom sidebar app are automatically linked to the Intercom conversation. This makes it easy to find the recording later from either Userplane or Intercom.

## Using Cmd+K instead

<Tip>
  If you prefer keyboard shortcuts, you can also create recording links using the Intercom Cmd+K
  action menu. Press **Cmd+K** (or **Ctrl+K** on Windows), type **Userplane**, and follow the
  prompts. See [Intercom Shortcuts](/integrations/intercom-shortcuts) for details.
</Tip>

## Requirements

- The Intercom integration must be [connected](/integrations/intercom).
- The agent must be a member of the Userplane workspace.
- Domains must be [verified](/workspace/domain-verification) to appear in the domain dropdown.
