---
title: Intercom Inbox
description: Create recording links and view recordings from the Intercom inbox sidebar
---

After [connecting Intercom](/integrations/intercom), a Userplane sidebar app appears in your Intercom inbox. This app lets agents create recording links, view recordings, and associate them with conversations — all without leaving Intercom.

## Creating a recording link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a conversation">
    Open a conversation in the Intercom inbox and click the **Userplane** sidebar app.

    <Frame caption="Userplane sidebar in Intercom">
      <img src="/media/intercom/intercom-inbox/sidebar/intercom-sidebar.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the link details">
    Click **Create Link**, select a **project** and **domain** from the dropdowns, then click **Create**.

    <Frame caption="Create link form in sidebar">
      <img src="/media/intercom/intercom-inbox/sidebar/intercom-create-link-form-sidebar.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    The link is generated and ready to share. Copy it to your clipboard.

    <Frame caption="Link created in sidebar">
      <img src="/media/intercom/intercom-inbox/sidebar/intercom-created-link-sidebar.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to the customer">
    Paste the recording link into the conversation and send it to the customer.

    <Frame caption="Recording link sent to customer">
      <img src="/media/intercom/intercom-inbox/intercom-recording-link-sent.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing recordings

When a customer completes a recording, it appears in the conversation automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Recording requested">
    The customer clicks the link and completes the recording. You receive a notification in the conversation.

    <Frame caption="Recording link received">
      <img src="/media/intercom/intercom-inbox/intercom-recording-link-received.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the recording">

## Conversation linking

Recordings created through the Intercom sidebar are automatically linked to the conversation. This makes it easy to find the recording later from either Userplane or Intercom.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Issue resolved">
    Once you've reviewed the recording and resolved the issue, close the conversation as normal.

    <Frame caption="Issue resolved">
      <img src="/media/intercom/intercom-inbox/intercom-issue-resolved.png" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Post-resolution follow-up">

## Using Cmd+K instead

<Tip>
  If you prefer keyboard shortcuts, you can also create recording links using the Intercom Cmd+K
  action menu. Press **Cmd+K** (or **Ctrl+K** on Windows), type **Userplane**, and follow the
  prompts. See [Intercom Shortcuts](/integrations/intercom-shortcuts) for details.
</Tip>

## Requirements

- The Intercom integration must be [connected](/integrations/intercom).
- The agent must be a member of the Userplane workspace.
